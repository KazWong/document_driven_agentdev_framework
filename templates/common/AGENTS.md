# Project agent contract

Use the repository's `.agent` policy and the centralized `agentctl` workflow. Active documents imported into `.agent/work-items/<id>/documents` are the authoritative project inputs; the work-item manifest controls lifecycle, scope, risk, verification, rollback, and hardware policy. Before Git-related work, read `.agent/branching-policy.md`. Production changes require an approved document-bound plan, an isolated branch/worktree, a framework-created candidate commit, deterministic verification of that exact commit, independent AI review, automatic provider-neutral PR publication, and human merge approval. Preserve document/plan/evidence history, keep `.agent/work-items` and `.agent-context` out of Git, keep secrets and generated `.test-results` out of commits, and validate ROS projects with the external `ros2-humble` lane. Agents must not force-push, publish ad-hoc branches, bypass candidate-SHA checks, or merge protected branches.
